Decisions this plan implements: success criterion 5 and orchestrator decision 3 (fork layout and the no-GSD repo are oracle-recorded wherever the oracle implements them; only the parts it lacks are Rust-only).

Meaning of "oracle-recorded" here: the scenario is registered `Kind::Oracle`, so its oracle side runs in every gate and must match its target, whatever its class (a Parity case's target is its explicit expectation; a `fixed:` case's target is its recorded outcome, after its run has been shown to differ from its expectation; a `regression:` case's target is its recorded outcome, or its expectation when none is recorded). The completeness check never requires a `fixed:` class: 02-07's observation rule makes several HARN-06 cut scenarios Parity (the fork-layout, squash re-cut and no-GSD cuts are predicted Parity in 02-14), and they count as oracle-recorded. The synthetic entries in the two control tests need no particular class either.

Shell note: every command in this plan is bash.
